Why Climb Fly Camp?

Our instructors aren't just great pilots — they're experienced mentors, mountain guides, and passionate teachers. Each flight is tailored to the student's comfort and curiosity.

Safety First. Always.

We fly under the guidelines of the US Hang Gliding & Paragliding Association and the FAA's FAR 103 exemption for tandem instruction. Your safety and education are our top priorities.

Whether you're curious about flight or ready to begin your paragliding journey, Climb Fly Camp is here to guide you.
