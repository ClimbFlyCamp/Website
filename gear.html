Paragliding Gear

At Climb Fly Camp, we proudly offer gear from some of the most trusted and innovative brands in the world of paragliding. Whether you're just starting out or upgrading for high-performance flying, we’re here to help you choose the right equipment for your goals and flying style.

Brands We Carry

Niviuk

Known for their precision engineering and performance-focused gliders, Niviuk offers wings for all levels—from beginner-friendly to advanced cross-country designs. Their harnesses and accessories are also widely respected for comfort and safety.

Woody Valley

Italian-made harnesses that set the standard in ergonomic design, comfort, and durability. Woody Valley’s range includes everything from lightweight hike-and-fly to full-featured XC and competition harnesses.

Flow Paragliders

Australian innovation at its best. Flow produces high-quality wings and reserves that emphasize safety, handling, and progressive performance. A growing favorite among both new and seasoned pilots.

PHI

Led by the legendary Hannes Papesh, PHI is known for delivering precise handling and excellent passive safety. Their gliders offer smooth progression for students and top-tier performance for experienced pilots.

Dudek

Highly regarded for their paramotor wings and paragliding gear, Dudek combines technical craftsmanship with proven flight characteristics. A great choice for both foot-launched and motorized flight.

Naviter

Top-tier flight instruments and navigation tools. Naviter devices, like the Oudie series, are pilot favorites for reliable GPS, live tracking, and intuitive interfaces. Stay informed and in control every step of the way.

XC Accessories

We also carry essential cross-country accessories—flight instruments, radios, reserve parachutes, lightweight packs, and more—to support your progression into longer, more adventurous flights.

Need Help Choosing?

We've helped tons of pilots find gear that suits their progression and ambitions. Contact us for a personal consultation, demo flight opportunities, or custom packages. We'll help you gear up with confidence and clarity.
